Manali

The Beautiful Himalayan Escape

Discover Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh, surrounded by mountains, forests and scenic valleys. It is a popular destination for nature lovers, adventure seekers and travellers looking for a peaceful getaway.

🏔️ Places to Visit in Manali

Solang Valley

Enjoy beautiful mountain scenery and outdoor activities in the valley.

Old Manali

Explore peaceful streets, cafés and the natural beauty around Old Manali.

Hadimba Temple

Visit this historic temple surrounded by beautiful cedar forests.

Rohtang Region

Experience spectacular Himalayan landscapes and mountain scenery.

📍 Popular Places

  • Solang Valley
  • Rohtang Pass
  • Hadimba Temple
  • Old Manali
  • Vashisht Village

🎯 Things to Do

  • Enjoy mountain views
  • Explore local markets
  • Visit temples
  • Enjoy adventure activities
  • Explore beautiful valleys

📅 Best Time to Visit

March to June is popular for pleasant weather and sightseeing. Winter is also popular for experiencing snow-covered landscapes.
