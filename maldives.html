Maldives

Paradise in the Indian Ocean

Discover Maldives

The Maldives is a beautiful tropical destination known for its crystal-clear waters, white sandy beaches and peaceful islands. It is a wonderful place for relaxation, sightseeing and enjoying the natural beauty of the ocean.

🌊 Places to Visit in Maldives

Malé

Explore the capital city and discover local culture and attractions.

Maafushi Island

Enjoy beautiful beaches and explore island life.

Ari Atoll

Discover beautiful tropical scenery and marine life around the atoll.

Hulhumalé

Explore the island's beaches and enjoy its relaxed atmosphere.

📍 Popular Places

  • Malé
  • Maafushi Island
  • Hulhumalé
  • Ari Atoll
  • North Malé Atoll

🎯 Things to Do

  • Enjoy beautiful beaches
  • Explore coral reefs
  • Go snorkeling
  • Enjoy boat trips
  • Explore local islands

📅 Best Time to Visit

November to April is generally a popular period to visit the Maldives because of its relatively dry and sunny weather.
